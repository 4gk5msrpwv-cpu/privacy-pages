隐私政策（中文）简体中文

一、概述

KidsLearn 是一款面向家长的儿童学习管理工具，提供课程表、作业记录、习惯打卡、学习提醒与积分奖励等功能。本应用由个人开发者（Frank Zhou）独立开发，无自建服务器、无后台数据库。本政策说明我们如何处理（事实上是：不处理）你的信息。

二、我们收集哪些信息：不收集

KidsLearn 不主动收集任何个人信息。具体而言：

  • 无需注册、无需登录：不要求也不获取姓名、邮箱、手机号、通讯录、位置、相册、身份标识等信息。
  • 学习数据仅存于你的 iCloud：你创建的课程表、作业、习惯、提醒、积分与奖品等内容，保存在你本人 Apple ID 下的 iCloud 私人数据库（Apple CloudKit，容器 iCloud.com.frankzhou.KidLearn）。这些数据归你所有，由 Apple 保管，开发者无法访问、读取或导出。
  • 家庭共享与「孩子空间」：基于 Apple CloudKit 的 CKShare 实现，数据仅在你主动邀请的 Apple ID（通常是家庭成员）之间共享，共享范围完全由你控制，可随时停止共享。
  • 设备能力与系统权限：
    • 通知：用于展示你自行设定的学习提醒，仅在你开启后使用，不含任何广告或营销内容。其中「远程通知」仅用作 iCloud 数据同步的触发信号（静默通知），不会产生任何可见提示，也不承载任何内容。
    • 相册（系统照片选择器）：作业清单的「图片识字」功能通过 Apple 系统照片选择器（PhotosPicker）挑选图片，App 只会拿到你选中的那一张，无法浏览或访问相册中的其他照片，也无需你授予相册权限。文字识别由 Apple Vision 框架完全在设备端完成，图片与识别结果不上传任何服务器，识别后不保留原图。本应用不使用相机，也不申请相机权限。
    • 面容 ID / 触控 ID / 设备密码：仅在家长修改或重置「儿童设备锁 PIN」、以及关闭儿童模式时，用于本机身份验证。验证由 iOS 系统完成，App 不会收到、也无法读取或存储任何生物特征信息。
    • 剪贴板：仅在你主动点击「粘贴」按钮时读取一次剪贴板中的文字，用于生成作业清单；读取后即在设备端处理，不上传、不长期保存。除此之外本应用不会访问剪贴板。
    • 不申请的权限：本应用不申请、不使用定位、通讯录、日历、提醒事项、麦克风、相机、蓝牙跟踪（App 追踪透明度）等权限，工程内除上述项外无任何权限声明。

三、第三方服务、追踪与广告：无

  • 本应用不含任何第三方代码库或 SDK：工程不依赖任何第三方包（无 Swift Package、无 CocoaPods），也不含广告 SDK、数据分析 SDK（如 Firebase、友盟等）、崩溃统计、归因或推送营销组件。
  • 不追踪：不使用 IDFA，不申请 App 追踪透明度授权，不进行跨应用或跨网站追踪，不设置用于追踪的 Cookie、设备指纹或本地标识。
  • 不出售、不共享、不交易任何用户数据；本应用不参与任何形式的数据商业化，也不将数据用于广告投放或用户画像。
  • 应用发起的全部网络请求均指向 Apple 官方服务：iCloud（CloudKit）数据同步，以及 App Store 内购（StoreKit）。二者均由 Apple 按其自身隐私政策处理，详见 Apple 隐私政策。（应用内「隐私政策」「支持与反馈」入口在 Safari 中打开，不属于本应用发起的网络请求，也不向本应用回传任何信息。）

四、儿童（未成年人）信息保护

KidsLearn 定位为面向家长的管理工具，而非儿童向应用：所有内容由家长创建与管理。应用不提供聊天、评论、公开主页、内容推荐/发现、陌生人互动或用户生成内容的公开发布功能。

  • 我们不收集、不出售、不向第三方披露儿童个人信息。儿童相关的学习记录（昵称、头像、课程、作业、习惯、积分等）全部由家长录入，仅存储在家长自己的 iCloud 账户中，开发者不接触、不可见。
  • 无公开用户生成内容：家长录入的文字内容仅在家长通过 iCloud 隐私共享（CKShare）一对一邀请的家庭成员之间可见，不向公众开放、无陌生人可见渠道、无搜索或推荐机制，因此本应用不涉及公开用户生成内容（UGC）相关的社交风险。
  • 家长控制工具：应用提供「儿童设备锁（PIN 保护）」——开启后配置类功能被隐藏，退出需输入 PIN；修改或重置 PIN 需通过系统生物识别或设备密码验证。家长可借此限制儿童对设置与购买的访问。
  • 儿童 Apple ID：若家长把孩子空间共享给儿童 Apple ID，该账号依法由家长创建与管理，共享范围始终由家长掌控，可随时停止共享。
  • 关于家长同意：由于本应用完全不收集儿童个人信息，美国 COPPA、欧盟 GDPR（儿童条款）以及中国《儿童个人信息网络保护规定》所要求的「可验证家长同意」机制在本应用中不适用；我们也不需要为此收集家长身份信息。家长可随时删除任一「孩子空间」及其全部数据，删除即刻生效。
  • 我们遵循中国《个人信息保护法》《儿童个人信息网络保护规定》，并参照 COPPA、GDPR 对儿童数据的保护原则：最小必要、家长控制、不共享、不出售、不用于广告或画像。
  • 如家长希望查询、更正或彻底删除与儿童相关的信息，可在应用内直接操作，或发送邮件至 fathyzhou@qq.com，我们将提供协助。

五、数据存储、安全与删除

  • 全部数据保存在你的 Apple ID 对应的 iCloud 账户中，受 Apple 的加密与访问控制保护，我们没有任何服务端副本。
  • 卸载 App 不会自动删除 iCloud 中的数据（便于换机恢复）。如需彻底删除：在 iPhone/iPad 进入「设置 → 顶部 Apple ID → iCloud → 管理账户储存空间 → KidsLearn → 删除数据」；或在 App 内进入「我的 → 孩子空间」，删除对应空间；也可发邮件至 fathyzhou@qq.com 请求删除协助。
  • 少量界面偏好（如语言、展示设置）保存在设备本地（系统 UserDefaults），不会离开你的设备，也不与 iCloud 同步。
  • 请妥善保管你的 Apple ID 与设备锁屏密码，这是保护这些数据的主要手段。

六、购买与付款

KidsLearn 免费下载并提供本地 7 天试用；完整版为一次性买断（非订阅型内购，无自动续费）。所有支付由 Apple App Store（StoreKit）处理，我们不会收到或存储你的支付卡信息，也看不到你的完整账户信息。内购已开启「家人共享」，家庭成员无需重复购买。

七、政策更新

如本政策发生变更，我们会更新本页面并修改顶部的「生效日期」与版本号；若涉及重大变更（例如新增数据收集），将在应用内以显著方式提示。建议你定期查看本页面以获取最新内容。

八、联系我们

如你对本政策或数据处理有任何疑问、投诉或删除请求，请发送邮件至 fathyzhou@qq.com，我们通常在 3 个工作日内回复。

Privacy Policy (English)EN

1. Overview

KidsLearn is a study-management tool for parents, offering timetables, homework tracking, habit check-ins, reminders and a points/rewards system. It is developed by an independent developer (Frank Zhou) and has no self-hosted server and no backend database. This policy explains how we handle — in fact, do not handle — your information.

2. What We Collect: Nothing

KidsLearn does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No sign-up, no login. We do not request or receive your name, email, phone number, contacts, location, photos or any identifier.
  • Your data stays in your own iCloud. Timetables, homework, habits, reminders, points and rewards you create are stored in the private iCloud database of your own Apple ID (Apple CloudKit, container iCloud.com.frankzhou.KidLearn). The data belongs to you and is held by Apple; the developer cannot access, read or export it.
  • Family Sharing & child spaces. Built on Apple CloudKit CKShare. Data is shared only with Apple IDs you explicitly invite (normally family members); you control and can revoke sharing at any time.
  • Device capabilities and system permissions:
    • Notifications — used solely to deliver the study reminders you set yourself; no advertising or marketing content. "Remote notifications" are used only as a silent trigger for iCloud synchronisation and never display a visible alert.
    • Photos (system photo picker) — the homework "scan text (OCR)" feature picks an image through Apple's system photo picker (PhotosPicker). The app receives only the single image you select, cannot browse or access the rest of your library, and requires no photo-library permission. Recognition runs entirely on device via Apple's Vision framework; neither the image nor the result is ever uploaded, and the original image is not retained. The app does not use the camera and does not request camera permission.
    • Face ID / Touch ID / device passcode — used only to authenticate on this device when a parent changes or resets the Child Device Lock PIN, or turns child mode off. Authentication is performed by iOS; the app never receives, reads or stores any biometric data.
    • Pasteboard — read once, only when you actively tap "Paste", to build a homework list. The text is processed on device; it is never uploaded or retained. The app does not access the pasteboard at any other time.
    • Permissions we never request — no location, contacts, calendars, reminders, microphone, camera or App Tracking authorisation. Apart from the items above, the app declares no other permissions.

3. Third Parties, Tracking and Advertising: None

  • No third-party code at all — the project depends on no Swift Package, no CocoaPods and no third-party library. There is no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK (no Firebase or similar), no crash-reporting, attribution or push-marketing component.
  • No tracking — no IDFA, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, no tracking cookies, device fingerprinting or local identifiers.
  • We never sell, share or trade user data, and we do not engage in any form of data monetisation. No data is used for advertising or user profiling.
  • Every network request goes to Apple services only — iCloud (CloudKit) synchronisation and App Store In-App Purchase (StoreKit). Both are governed by Apple's own privacy policy — see Apple Privacy Policy. (The in-app "Privacy Policy" and "Support" entries open in Safari; they are not network requests made by the app and return no information to it.)

4. Children's Privacy

KidsLearn is a parent-facing management tool, not an app made for children: all content is created and managed by parents. The app offers no chat, comments, public profiles, content discovery or recommendation, interaction with strangers, or public sharing of user-generated content.

  • We do not collect, sell or disclose children's personal information to any third party. Children's study records (nickname, avatar, courses, homework, habits, points, etc.) are entered by parents and stored only in the parent's own iCloud account; the developer has no access to them.
  • No public user-generated content. Text entered by parents is visible only to family members the parent invites one-to-one via iCloud private sharing (CKShare). There is no public audience, no stranger visibility, and no search or recommendation mechanism, so the app carries none of the social risks associated with public UGC.
  • Parental controls. The app provides a "Child Device Lock (PIN)": once enabled, configuration features are hidden and exiting requires the PIN; changing or resetting the PIN requires system biometric or passcode authentication. Parents can use it to restrict children's access to settings and purchases.
  • Children's Apple IDs. If a parent shares a child space with a child Apple ID, that account is created and managed by the parent under Apple's rules; sharing remains under the parent's control and can be stopped at any time.
  • On parental consent. Because the app collects no children's personal information at all, the "verifiable parental consent" mechanisms required by COPPA (US), GDPR (children's provisions, EU) and China's Regulations on the Protection of Children's Personal Information Online do not apply, and we do not need to collect any parent identity information for that purpose. Parents may delete any child space and all of its data at any time; deletion takes effect immediately.
  • We follow China's Personal Information Protection Law and the Regulations on the Protection of Children's Personal Information Online, and align with COPPA / GDPR principles: data minimisation, parental control, no sharing, no selling, and no advertising or profiling.
  • Parents who wish to review, correct or permanently delete child-related information can do so inside the app, or email fathyzhou@qq.com for assistance.

5. Data Storage, Security and Deletion

  • All data resides in the iCloud account of your Apple ID, protected by Apple's encryption and access controls. We keep no server-side copy.
  • Deleting the app does not automatically remove iCloud data (so you can restore on a new device). To erase everything: on iPhone/iPad go to Settings → your Apple ID → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → KidsLearn → Delete Data; delete the corresponding child space inside the app under Me → Child Spaces; or email fathyzhou@qq.com to request deletion assistance.
  • A small number of interface preferences (language, display settings) are stored locally on the device (system UserDefaults). They never leave your device and are not synced to iCloud.
  • Please keep your Apple ID and device passcode secure — they are the primary protection for this data.

6. Purchases and Payments

KidsLearn is free to download with a local 7-day trial. The full version is a one-time purchase (non-consumable, non-subscription; no auto-renewal). All payments are processed by the Apple App Store (StoreKit). We never receive or store your payment card details, nor can we see your full account information. Family Sharing is enabled, so family members do not need to purchase again.

7. Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Effective Date" and version shown at the top. Material changes (for example, introducing any new data collection) will also be announced prominently inside the app. Please review this page periodically for the latest version.

8. Contact Us

For any question, complaint or deletion request regarding this policy or your data, email fathyzhou@qq.com. We normally reply within 3 business days.
