🛡 Privacy Policy

1. Overview

Famora is a family health diary for recording blood pressure, blood glucose, weight and daily medication for family members (for example, elderly parents). It is developed by an independent developer (Frank Zhou) and has no self-hosted server and no backend database. This policy explains how we handle — in fact, do not handle — your information.

2. What We Collect: Nothing

Famora does not collect any personal information. Specifically:

  • No sign-up, no login. We do not request or receive your name, email, phone number, contacts, location or any identifier.
  • All data stays on this device. Readings, medication plans, profiles and reminders are stored in the app's local on-device database (Apple SwiftData). The data never leaves the device unless you explicitly export or share a report (for example, the doctor-visit PDF you choose to share) or enable the optional iCloud family sharing described below. The developer has no access to it.
  • Optional iCloud family sharing (Premium only). Sharing profiles with family members is part of Famora Premium and is always opt-in and invitation-based: it uses your own Apple iCloud (CloudKit) to share profiles with the family members you explicitly invite — for example, so adult children can view an elderly parent's readings, or so a senior can use the app in Senior Mode without any setup. With Auto Sync (an in-app toggle, also under your control), new readings and medication check-ins upload automatically to iCloud within seconds while you use the app and whenever it goes to the background. Invited family members receive a health summary (today's readings and medication adherence) in the app's Message Center, and a local notification when new summaries arrive while the app refreshes in the background. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple's infrastructure; it is never stored on any Famora server, and the developer has no access to shared content. Sharing is off by default and starts only after you create an invitation in the app; you can turn off Auto Sync or stop sharing at any time.

Device capabilities and system permissions:

  • Apple Health (HealthKit) — with your explicit permission, the app can write readings you record (blood pressure, glucose, weight) to the Health app, and read blood pressure, glucose and weight samples to import them (for example, from a paired blood-pressure monitor). Health data is processed on device and written to/ read from Apple's Health store only. The developer never receives, sees or stores any health data. You can grant or revoke each permission at any time in the Health app.
  • iCloud (CloudKit) — accessed only if you enable the Premium family sharing described above; otherwise no iCloud connection is made. CloudKit transport and storage are operated and encrypted by Apple under Apple's own privacy policy.
  • Photos Picker (optional profile avatar) — if you choose to set a profile photo, selection happens entirely through Apple's system Photos Picker: only the single image you explicitly pick is copied, downscaled and stored in the app's local database (and shared with the profile when the optional iCloud family sharing is enabled). The rest of your photo library is never read, and photos are never uploaded to any server. You can remove the avatar at any time in the profile editor.
  • Microphone & Speech Recognition (optional voice input) — with your explicit permission, you can dictate readings instead of typing. Audio is processed on device by Apple's speech recognition in real time, is never recorded to disk, stored or uploaded, and no audio ever reaches the developer.
  • Apple Watch app (optional companion) — Famora ships with an optional companion Apple Watch app. It shares the same database as the iPhone app through your own iCloud account, so readings recorded on the watch are exactly the data described above. The watch app makes no independent network connections of its own and collects nothing by itself; you can remove it from the watch at any time.
  • Notifications — local notifications only, used solely to deliver the measurement and medication reminders you set yourself, and optional family health summaries delivered as local notifications when the app refreshes in the background (iOS background app refresh). No advertising or marketing content, and no push infrastructure of our own.
  • Permissions we never request — no location, contacts, camera, calendar or App Tracking authorisation.

3. Third Parties, Tracking and Advertising: None

  • No third-party code — the app has no advertising SDK, no analytics SDK (no Firebase or similar), no crash-reporting, attribution or push-marketing component.
  • No tracking — no IDFA, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no cross-app or cross-site tracking, no device fingerprinting.
  • We never sell, share or trade user data, and we do not engage in any form of data monetisation.
  • Purchases — the optional Premium subscription is processed entirely by Apple's StoreKit. The developer never sees your payment details or any personal identifier; Apple's own privacy policy applies to the transaction.
  • Apart from Apple services (HealthKit and StoreKit), the only network request the app may make is loading this privacy/support page in the built-in reader (no address bar, no cookies, no identifiers, navigation to other domains blocked). The page is hosted on a third-party static host; loading it carries no account or device information.

4. Medical Disclaimer

Famora is a record-keeping and organisation tool for families, not a medical device and not a regulated diagnostic product. It does not measure anything itself: all readings are entered by the user or imported from devices paired with Apple Health. Statistics (averages, counts, trend charts, target-range flags) are data descriptions only and do not constitute medical conclusions, diagnosis or treatment advice. Always consult a qualified physician before making health decisions.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

  • Your data exists only in the local database of this device.
  • You can erase all measurement and medication data at any time in Settings → Profile → Clear all measurement & medication data.
  • Deleting a profile removes it from the list; measurements recorded under it remain in the local database until cleared as above, or until the app is uninstalled, which removes the entire local database.

6. Children's Privacy

Famora is a caregiver-facing diary intended for adults recording health readings of family members (such as elderly parents). It is not directed at children, offers no chat, comments, public profiles, discovery, recommendations or public sharing of user-generated content, and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone — including children.

7. Changes to This Policy

When we add or change features that involve data (such as the optional iCloud family sharing described above), we update this policy and show the change in the app. The effective date at the top of this page always reflects the latest version.

8. Contact

Questions or requests about privacy: Your.Famora@outlook.com. We usually reply within 48 hours.

💬 Support & FAQ

Where is my data stored?

On this device only, in the app's local database. There is no server and no account.

Can family members see each other's data?

Profiles stay on this device unless optional iCloud family sharing (Premium) is enabled. Family sharing is invitation-based, off by default, and runs entirely through your own Apple iCloud (CloudKit) — no Famora server is involved, and the developer has no access. Anyone with access to the unlocked device can always open the app; use iOS Screen Time / passcode features if you need device-level protection.

How does family sharing sync work?

Once you invite family members, Auto Sync uploads new readings and medication check-ins to iCloud automatically — within seconds while you use the app, and whenever it goes to the background. Your family sees the latest data the next time they open the app, receives a health summary in the in-app Message Center, and — when the app refreshes in the background — a local notification. Auto Sync is a toggle in Settings and can be turned off anytime; syncing runs entirely through your own iCloud.

Is there an Apple Watch app?

Yes — Famora includes a companion Apple Watch app. After installing Famora on your iPhone, add it to the watch from the Watch app. The senior can log blood pressure, glucose and medication check-ins right from the wrist, and the data syncs with the iPhone automatically through your own iCloud. The watch app is optional and collects nothing by itself.

How do I set the app up for an elderly family member?

First finish the setup yourself (profiles, target ranges, reminders), then turn on Senior Mode in Settings → Family Sharing. Senior Mode uses larger text and hides advanced settings, so the senior can simply record readings without touching any configuration.

How do I import data from my blood-pressure monitor?

Pair your device with Apple Health (many Omron and similar monitors do this), then in Famora open Settings → Health → Import last 30 days. Readings appear on the Home and Trends tabs.

How do I share a report with my doctor?

Open the Report tab, choose a period, tap Preview Report, check the PDF and tap the share button. The PDF is generated on device and only leaves the phone when you share it.

The app shows a value in mmol/L but my device uses mg/dL (or vice versa).

Open Settings → Target ranges → Glucose unit and switch between mg/dL and mmol/L. Internally all values are stored in mg/dL; switching only changes how values are shown and entered.

Can I use pounds instead of kilograms for weight?

Yes — open Settings → Target ranges → Weight unit and switch between kg and lb. The default follows your app language (English defaults to lb, other languages to kg). All values are stored internally in kilograms; switching only changes display and input, and history is converted automatically.

I deleted a profile by mistake. Can I recover it?

Profiles can be re-created in seconds from Settings → Profile → Add profile. Historical readings are not deleted with the profile; clear them explicitly if you want to.
